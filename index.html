My Stance

Good product language is evidence of good product thinking. It shows the user what changed, what matters, and what they can safely do next.

In AI products, that means designing for calibrated trust: clear limits, visible reasoning, graceful failure, and user control instead of vague promises of magic.

I help companies navigate the transition to AI, showing teams how to combine generative tools with strict content guidelines and human-in-the-loop editing to preserve user trust.

Annika's Portfolio Assistant
What disciplines does Annika work across?
Three areas: UX Writing, UX Design, and UX Research. She can write the product copy, shape the interaction flow, and run the research that validates both. Her UX Writing section covers work at Capgemini, Microsoft, and British Council. Her design and research case studies focus on pet care and postpartum wellness apps.
Can she work on AI products?
Yes. Her strongest AI work is about trust calibration: onboarding that explains what the system can and can't do, fallback states that preserve momentum, and research that shows when confidence helps or hurts.

Design for calibrated trust

I make system limits, confidence, next steps, and user control visible, especially when AI is involved or the stakes are high.

Leave a system behind

About

Annika Bhargava

UX Writer and AI Enablement Strategist with 8+ years shaping how products talk to people. I've spent my career at the intersection of language and technology, writing the words that help people trust, understand, and actually use the products they interact with every day.

I believe the best UX writing is invisible. You don't notice it. You just know what to do next. In the age of generative AI, that clarity is the difference between adoption and churn.

I partner with product teams to build clear conversation structures, error message frameworks, and AI trust guidelines. I also help organizations scale their AI copy processes, building customized content playbooks and training internal teams to run efficient, high-quality human-in-the-loop workflows.

I've worked across consulting, edtech, and enterprise software—from establishing content standards at Microsoft to driving conversion at Capgemini and guiding AI workflows at the British Council.
